Overdrive

v0.2.1

ROG Ally controls on Windows: performance modes with their own watts, Auto TDP, FPS limit, CPU and GPU fan curves, CPU Boost, Windows power mode and Auto SR. Controller, vibration and touch settings are in Traction. Unofficial; based on G-Helper. Needs Decky Loader for Windows.

Clarity

v0.1.1

Per-game OptiScaler on Windows: install it into a game, pick the AMD FSR 4 model (Lite, Standard or Auto) and turn frame generation on or off, then remove it cleanly. Uses the FSR 4 files from your AMD driver. Warns about anti-cheat games; don't use it in online games. Packs a pre-release OptiScaler build. Unofficial. Needs Decky Loader for Windows.

optiscalerfsrupscalingframe-generation
GitHubDownload v0.1.1

Traction

v0.1.0

ROG Ally controls on Windows: controller mode (Gamepad, Desktop or Auto), grip vibration, touchscreen on or off, and the Armoury Crate switch shared with Overdrive. Works with or without Overdrive (0.2.0 or newer). Unofficial; based on G-Helper. Needs Decky Loader for Windows.

asusrog-allycontroller
GitHubDownload v0.1.0
